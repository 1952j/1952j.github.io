Giving Birth on a Yellow Bed...

  On the day I was born, September 29, in one of the old houses of Damascus, the earth, too, was in labor... and autumn was preparing to unpack its yellow suitcases ..

 The earth and my mother carried us at the same time... and brought us into the world at the same time ..

 Was it merely a coincidence, I wonder, that my birth coincided with the season in which the earth revolts against itself and trees shed all their old garments?
Or was I destined to be like October – a month of change and transformations?

 All I know is that on the day I was born, nature was staging its coup against summer... calling upon the fields, the grass, the flowers, and the birds to join its rebellion... against the routine of the earth ..
