My Birthday

You still ask me about my birthday?

Then write down what you do not yet know:

The day your love for me began
is the day that I was born…

You, the one with the black eyes,
with those wakeful, rain-kissed eyes…

I ask nothing of my Lord but two things:

to protect those two eyes,

and to add two more days to my life…
so that I may write poetry

about those two pearls…
