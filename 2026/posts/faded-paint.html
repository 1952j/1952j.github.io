Faded Paint

The female body is a somewhat foolish chemical recording device.

Every time she opens the gate to a new man, glue called oxytocin is poured into her brain!!

This glue wasn’t made to be handed out to passersby… rather, it was designed to bind her to one man and one man only, like an old piece of chewing gum stuck to your shoe that never comes off.

Yet she has poured that glue onto twenty different shoes.

Every time she said ‘I miss you’ or ‘I love you,’ the glue drained away, drop by drop.

Until the tank finally ran empty 👈 the chewing gum became a faded, sticky mess, like a piece of gum that had been chewed for an entire year, then stuck under a chair and forgotten.

So what do you, the next man, end up finding?!

You don't find a mature "woman"… instead, you find a peeling wall covered in layers of cheap paint in every imaginable color.

He touches her, and no storm arises.

He looks into her eyes, and sees no spark.

He speaks, and his words pass through her like the static of an old radio, where nothing is heard except the buzz.

Her body remembers old signals far better than it remembers his own face.

And feminism applauds, shouting ‘Empowerment!! Freedom!! Independence!!’ while in reality, it was draining the tank and calling the process ‘maturity.’

Then she is surprised when the marriage collapses after two years, when the man grows bored, and when she herself keeps chasing the next thrill despite already having him.

The answer does not lie in ‘patriarchal society.’

The answer is that the glue has run out, the chewing gum has lost its flavor, and the wall can no longer hold anything.

And the man who understands the game doesn't get angry.

He smiles a cold smile and quietly steps away.

He looks for a wall that has only ever been painted once.

Because once the glue runs out… it never comes back.
