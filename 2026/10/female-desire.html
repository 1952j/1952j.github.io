The Economics of Female Desire?

Why does a woman abandon all her standards for the man she desires, yet put up a thousand barriers for an ordinary man?

This has nothing to do with whether a woman is conservative, liberal, or anything else. Rather, it is an ancient biological law that philosophers discussed long before modern psychology existed.

A woman is not a rational creature searching for truth; she is an agent of nature. Nature wants a child with the best possible genes. Therefore, a woman does not simply choose a man; but rather she chooses the potential father of her children.

When she perceives superiority (good looks, height, strength) or social superiority (wealth, status, confidence), she drops all her requirements, because the maternal instinct is stronger than any logic.

A woman is the most cautious creature when it comes to choosing a man.

She does not love without a reason; she tests. She sets up barriers not out of hatred for the poor or the unattractive, but because she sees no evolutionary value in them. Yet when it comes to an alpha male, those barriers fall because he passes the test without a word.

She deals with men through a system of reward and punishment. She offers attention and intimacy as a reward to the man who provides her with security and resources, and withholds them as a punishment to the man who achieves nothing.

This is why you see her extremely kind to the wealthy man and very cold to the poor man. The same woman, but two different sets of standards.

⏺️ A woman is not evil, but her instincts is 100% selective and interest-driven.

The problem is not that these instincts exist. The problem arises when we deny their existence and call it “unconditional love.”
