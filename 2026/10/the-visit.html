The Visit

Where did you come in from?
And how did you find your way to me,
when my face is as cold and expressionless
as any wall?

I wonder:

How could a woman like you
seek an encounter with a wall?

I will tell you with complete honesty
that I write love poetry…
But I…
am one of the worst kinds of lovers…
for I do not know how to love…

And all my miracles…
are fabrications of the news agencies!

My lady:
Who gave you the idea
that I am Samson the Mighty?

It is not true…
that I have mastered the art of magic,
or that I leap over fire.

It is not true…
that I whisper into the ears of two breasts,
and rivers begin to flow between them…

It is not true…
that when I touch a woman's waist,
an electric current shocks me…

It is not true…
that when I hold a woman against my chest,
trees begin to grow within my chest…

It is not true…
that when I gaze into your dark eyes,
lightning flashes…
and rain begins to pour…

My lady:
Do not listen to your friends…
when they tell you
that I am the king of romance…

For all my heroic exploits…
are fabrications of the news agencies…
