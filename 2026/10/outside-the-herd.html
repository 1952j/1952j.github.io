Outside the Herd

People do not always love you for who you truly are…
They love the version of you that does not unsettle them.

When you are ordinary, you are understandable…
easy to read. You threaten no one, and awaken nothing that lies dormant within them.

But when you become exceptional…
you do not merely rise; you shine a light on those who never did.

You become a harsh mirror in which others see what they could have been… but failed to become.

At that point, criticism is less about hating you and more about an internal conflict within themselves.

For excellence exposes complacency, and distinction embarrasses the ordinary.

The philosopher Friedrich Nietzsche is said to have observed:

“Everything that rises high stirs the winds around itself.”

And so…

when you are criticized simply because you have become different,
know that you have stepped outside the herd…

and the herd always fears the wolf,
not because the herd is weaker…
but because the wolf is not like them.
