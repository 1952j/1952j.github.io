The Resting Boat

The journey had weighed heavily upon it, and it bowed.

It was not surrender, but a moment of listening
to the sea as it calmed at sunset.

The sea did not ask it to remain upright,
only to lay down what it had carried…

and to watch the sun sink alone,
this time.
