After the Ball

By Leo Tolstoy

Ivan was a young man living the happiest days of his life.

One evening, he attended a lavish ball. There, he fell in love with a beautiful girl named Varenka.

What impressed him most was her father, a high-ranking army officer. Ivan saw in him the embodiment of nobility, kindness, and dignity.

The officer danced with his daughter as everyone looked on in admiration. He appeared to be a loving father and a refined gentleman.

Ivan returned home filled with happiness, but he couldn't sleep. So he went out for a walk as dawn broke.

There, he witnessed a scene that would change his life forever.

He saw soldiers punishing a fellow soldier who had attempted to escape. The man was being brutally flogged.

And the officer overseeing the torture was none other than Varenka’s father himself.

The very man who had seemed a symbol of grace and mercy at the ball.

He was now mercilessly ordering the beating to continue.

Ivan stood frozen in shock.

He could not reconcile the two images.

How could a single person be so gentle in the evening and so cruel at dawn?

From that day onward, his perspective on the world, authority, and society as a whole was forever transformed.
