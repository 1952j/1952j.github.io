The Transient Woman

Love what you do not see twice…

The most magnificent of women is the transient one…
the woman who crosses your life without asking for a place within it.

She leaves a small disturbance within you, awakens something that had been sleeping, then vanishes before her presence can become a routine, and her beauty can become part of the furniture…

The passing woman does not give you enough time to uncover her flaws, nor does she allow herself the chance to discover your triviality.

She remains suspended in your memory… not as she truly is, but as reality failed to corrupt her!

As for the woman who chooses to linger and lie in wait, she gradually begins to occupy the space you once granted her.

Closeness turns into an obligation, attention into surveillance, and love into a claim of ownership over you.

Little by little, she grows tired of you. She no longer wants you, yet at the same time, she is seized by a strange, petty desire to possess even your ability to leave!

To this day, I still do not understand this contradiction…

The moment she feels secure that you will not walk away, her need to remain beautiful in your eyes simply falls away.

Without question… the most magnificent woman is the transient one.

The woman who passes through only to spark a flash of fleeting attraction, and then leaves.

While the one who lingers, who refuses to ever leave… binds your breath until you suffocate.

Love is a fleeting moment…
and it becomes ugly when it lies in wait!
