The Illusion of Regret

Regret… the biggest trick the mind believes.

You think you are suffering because of what you did.
You think it is the past that hurts you.

But the past no longer exists.

What exists now is a thought saying:

“I shouldn't have done that.”

You believe it.
You repeat it.
And you give it a power and a reality it does not possess.

Look deeply.

Regret is not a wound.
It is a story the mind tells itself over and over again.

Before this thought… who are you?
And after this thought… who are you?

The awareness that observes regret has never regretted anything.

It is simply present…
Silent…
Complete, just as it is.

The moment you stop believing the mind's story,
regret begins to disappear on its own.

And all that remains is what has always been there:

You… before every story.
